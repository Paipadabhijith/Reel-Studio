# Gully Se Global Takht Tak: Cricket Ne India Ko Kaise Jeeta

## Long-form

### Ek angrezi khel, Hindustani zameen

[1] Har gali mein ek pitch hota hai, jise kisi ne mark nahi kiya. Teen eent ke stumps, aur ek lakdi ka bat.
[2] Ball? Tennis ball par electrical tape. Rules? Local law. Ball padosi ki khidki par lagi, toh game over.
[3] Ye kahani hai ek angrezi gaon ke khel ki, jo ek arab logon ki zubaan ban gaya.
[4] Lekin shuru karne ke liye humein wahan jaana hoga, jab India ka sabse bada khel cricket tha hi nahi.
[5] 1721 mein Cambay ke samundar kinare, East India Company ke sailors ne India ka pehla recorded match khela.
[6] Phir 1792 mein Calcutta Cricket Club bana. Hindustaniyon ka kaam sirf ball uthakar dena tha.
[7] Phir Parsiyon ne kahani badli. 1848 mein Bombay mein Oriental Cricket Club bana, Indians ka pehla club.
[8] 1886 mein Parsi team England gayi. Zyada match haari, par ek line paar ho chuki thi.
[9] Ranjitsinhji ne England ke liye apne pehle Test mein 154 not out thok diya. Unke naam par Ranji Trophy hai.
[10] Ek taraf mahal the, doosri taraf galiyan. 1912 tak Bombay ka Quadrangular bhari bheed kheench raha tha.
[11] Wahan ek ghulam kaum, kuch ghanton ke liye, apne hukmaranon ke barabar khadi thi.
[12] Shuruaat mein ye gentleman ka khel tha. Paanch din ka Test, lunch, tea, aur draw bhi ho sakta tha.
[13] India ne ise apna accent diya. Dheeli pitch, spin ka jaadu, aur kalaai ka kamaal.
[14] 1932 mein Lord's par India ka pehla Test hua. 158 runs se haare, par ek desh ne entry maar di.

### Jab hockey ka raj tha

[15] Aaj ke bahut se log nahi jaante, par ek zamaane mein duniya ki sabse badi team hamari hockey team thi.
[16] 1928, Amsterdam. India ka pehla Olympic gold. Dhyan Chand ki stick par vipaksh bole, isme chumbak laga hai.
[17] 1936, Berlin. Final mein host Germany ko 8-1 se rounda. Dhyan Chand ban gaye The Wizard.
[18] Azaadi ke baad bhi raj chala: 1948, 1952, 1956. Lagataar chhe Olympic gold.
[19] Us zamaane mein kisi se poochte ki desh ka khel kaun sa hai, toh jawab hota: hockey.
[20] Toh hockey ka raja desh cricket ka pujari kaise ban gaya? Koi ek din nahi tha, ye dheere dheere khisakna tha.
[21] Pehli wajah: cricket sasta tha. Ek bat, ek ball, ek deewar. Bas, game shuru.
[22] Doosri: cricket mein hero banta hai. Ek bowler, ek batsman, poora desh dekh raha. Averages aur records par bahas chalti rehti.
[23] Teesri: radio. All India Radio ki commentary ne Test match ko poore desh ka saajha soundtrack bana diya.
[24] Phir 1976 mein Montreal Olympics mein artificial turf aaya. Europe aur Australia ne apna liya, India ke liye mehenga pad gaya.
[25] Desh ne hockey se pyaar karna nahi chhoda. Bas dekhna chhod diya. Paisa aur hero cricket ki taraf khisakte gaye.

[27] 1971 mein West Indies aur England dono jagah series jeet. Gavaskar ne us series mein 774 runs banaye.
[28] Par one-day cricket mein hum mazaak the. 1975 World Cup mein Gavaskar ne 60 over khel kar 36 not out banaye.
[29] 1983 aate aate umeedein lagbhag zero thi. Bookmakers ke hisaab se India ke chances sirf 66 to 1.

### TV, paisa aur BCCI

[37] 83 ne aag lagayi, par aag ko eendhan chahiye tha. Pehla eendhan: television.
[38] 1982 Asian Games ke saath colour TV aaya, aur Doordarshan poore desh ka campfire ban gaya.
[39] 1989 mein sirf solah saal ka ladka, Sachin Tendulkar, Pakistan mein debut karta hai. Khoon ponchta hai, aur khelta rehta hai.
[40] Dusra eendhan: 1991 ki liberalisation. Satellite channels, sponsors, advertisers aaye, aur sabko chahiye the audience.
[41] Aur cricket ke paas desh ki sabse badi audience thi.
[42] Teesra: Jagmohan Dalmiya. Calcutta ka businessman, jisne dekha ki asli taaqat broadcasting rights mein hai.
[43] 1995 mein Supreme Court ne kaha, airwaves public property hain. Doordarshan ka monopoly khatam.
[44] Ab BCCI apne match sabse ooncha bolne walon ko bech sakta tha.
[45] 1996 World Cup commercial hit raha, aur 1997 mein Dalmiya ICC ke president ban gaye.
[46] England aur Australia ke paas history thi. India ke paas ek arab dekhne waale the.
[47] Par saaya bhi tha. 2000 ka match-fixing scandal. Phir bhi fans wapas aaye, aur board ko apni taaqat ka andaza ho gaya.

### IPL Revolution

[48] 2007 World Cup mein India pehle round mein hi bahar. Poore desh mein maatam.
[49] Kuch mahino baad, Dhoni ki naya-naya team ne pehla T20 World Cup jeet liya, Pakistan ko last over mein haraakar.
[50] Udhar Zee ke Indian Cricket League ne BCCI ko challenge kiya. Board ka jawab tha: Lalit Modi aur IPL.
[51] 2008. Aath city franchises, business houses aur film stars ke maalik, auction, cheerleaders, floodlights.
[52] 18 April 2008, Bangalore. McCullum ne 158 not out thoka, aur cricket ek tyohaar ban gaya.
[53] Pehla champion? Rajasthan Royals, Shane Warne ki captaincy mein, aakhri ball par Chennai ko haraakar.
[54] Paisa: broadcast rights lagbhag ek billion dollar se badhkar takriban 6 billion dollar tak pahunch gaye.

[57] Toofan bhi aaye. 2013 ka spot-fixing, do franchises suspend, Modi ki giraavat, court ke reforms.
[58] Phir bhi IPL tikka raha. Itna bada ho chuka tha ki girna mumkin nahi tha.
