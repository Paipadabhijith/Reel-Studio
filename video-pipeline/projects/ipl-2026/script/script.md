[3] Kehte hain 2025 winning ka saal tha, aur 2026 domination ka. Aaj hum dekhenge ki yeh line kitni sahi hai, aur kitni nahi.

### 2025: Winning Ka Saal

[7] Lekin pehle thoda peeche chalte hain, 2025 mein. Us saal RCB ne final sirf chhe runs se jeeta tha. Margin bahut patla tha.
[8] Woh jeet pehli IPL trophy thi. Saalon ka intezaar, saalon ke meme, aur har saal ek hi line: is baar nahi hua, agli baar.
[9] Har saal kahani wahi hoti thi. Team strong dikhti, fans ummeed lagate, aur phir kisi ek bure match mein sapna toot jata tha.
[10] 2025 mein woh chakra toota. Bengaluru ke fans ke liye woh sirf ek jeet nahi thi, ek bojh ka utar jana tha.
[11] Aur sach batao, us din har fan ne bhi ek sawaal socha. Kya yeh ek lucky season tha, ya ek aisi team ki shuruaat?
[12] Jab tak 2026 nahi aaya, is sawaal ka jawab kisi ke paas nahi tha. Sirf ummeed thi, aur thodi si ghabrahat bhi.
[13] Kyunki trophy jeetne ke baad ek naya pressure aata hai. Ab aap underdog nahi ho, ab har team aapko haraana chahti hai.
[14] Defending champion hona ek bhaari tag hai. Zyadatar teams us pressure mein bikhar jaati hain, aur season ke beech mein hi ladkhadane lagti hain.
[15] Lekin RCB ne is baar alag script likhi. Season ke end tak woh final tak pahunche, aur wahan unka saamna hua Gujarat Titans se.
[16] Gujarat ke liye yeh unka teesra IPL final tha. Matlab dono teams ke paas bharosa tha, aur dono ke paas kuch sabit karne ko tha.

### Final 2026: Ahmedabad Ki Raat

[17] Ab chalte hain final ki raat par. Toss RCB ne jeeta, aur unhone pehle field karne ka faisla liya. Bada call tha.
[18] Final mein pehle batting karna aksar safe maana jaata hai. Lekin RCB ne chase pe bharosa dikhaya, aur yahi bharosa unki pehchan ban gaya.
[19] Shuruaat hi tight rahi. Josh Hazlewood aur Bhuvneshwar Kumar ne Gujarat ke top order ko jaldi hi hilakar rakh diya.
[20] Shubman Gill aur Sai Sudharsan, jinpe Gujarat ki poori batting tiki thi, powerplay ke andar hi pavilion laut gaye.
[21] Washington Sundar ne ek end sambhala aur adhi-shatak banaya. Unhone 37 gend mein naabad pachaas run banaye, jo ek solid knock tha.
[22] Phir bhi Gujarat kabhi khul ke nahi khel paayi. Regular wickets girte rahe, aur woh poore 20 overs mein sirf 155 par 8 tak pahunche.
[23] RCB ke gendbaazon mein Rasikh Salam Dar chamke. Unhone 27 run dekar teen wicket nikaale, aur Gujarat ki ummeed todne mein bada role nibhaya.
[24] Bhuvneshwar Kumar aur Hazlewood ne bhi do-do wicket liye. Yeh ek poora team effort tha, sirf ek star ka show nahi.
[25] Target tha 156. T20 final ke hisaab se chhota lagta hai, lekin final ka pressure score ko bada bana deta hai.
[26] Aur is pressure ke beech jo aadmi khada tha, woh tha Virat Kohli. Aise hi pal ke liye toh fans ne saalon intezaar kiya tha.

[30] Aur phir aakhir mein, ek chhakka. Kohli ne apne trademark style mein chase khatam kiya, aur RCB paanch wicket se jeet gaye.
[31] Is jeet ke saath RCB back-to-back champion ban gaye. Jeetne wali team ko 20 crore mile, aur Gujarat ko saade bahara crore.

### Winning vs Domination

[33] Ab asli sawaal par aate hain. Kya 2026 sach mein domination tha, ya bas ek aur close jeet? Chalo numbers se samjhte hain.
[34] 2025 final mein RCB ne 190 banaye aur chhe run se jeete. 2026 mein unhone 161 ka score banaya aur chase karke jeete.
[35] Dekha? Dono finals ke scorelines bahut kareeb ke the. Toh agar domination ka matlab bade margin se jeetna hai, toh yeh poora sach nahi hai.
[36] Lekin domination ka ek aur matlab bhi hota hai. Woh ki aap pressure mein bhi apni cheezein sahi karo, baar baar, bina ghabraye.
[37] 2025 mein RCB ne jeet haasil ki. 2026 mein unhone ek aur kaam kiya: woh jeet ko defend kar paaye, jo bahut kam teams kar paati hain.
[38] Isi liye hum kehte hain ki 2025 winning ka saal tha. Woh ek sapna poora hone ka saal tha, ek bhaari intezaar ka ant.
[39] Aur 2026 domination ka saal isliye tha, kyunki ab RCB ko koi lucky ya fluke nahi keh sakta. Ab woh hi champion hain.
[40] Vaise IPL 2026 ka Orange Cap Vaibhav Sooryavanshi ne jeeta. Yaani naye youngsters bhi aa gaye hain, aur lead lene ko tayyar hain.
[41] Yeh bhi yaad rakhna ki RCB ne jis team ko haraya, woh koi kamzor nahi thi. Gujarat Titans bhi ek strong, tough side thi.
[42] Gujarat ke Ahmedabad mein hi, Karnataka ki team ne jeet ka jhanda gaada. Dono rajyon ka ek hi stadium mein aamna-saamna tha.
