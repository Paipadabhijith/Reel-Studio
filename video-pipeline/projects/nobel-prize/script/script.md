# Nobel Chemistry 2026: Mirror Molecules Explained

## reel_1

Read these in order for this reel's narration:

[1] Aapke dono haath ek doosre ke mirror image hain, par ek ke upar doosra fit nahi hota. Molecules ke saath bhi yahi hota hai.
[2] Aur isi idea pe Henri Kagan aur Kenso Soai ko mila 2026 ka Chemistry Nobel.
[3] Aise molecules ko kehte hain chiral, Greek word se, jiska matlab hai haath.
[4] Nature mein zyada tar living cheezein bas ek hi version use karti hain, doosra version almost gayab hai.
[5] Ye sawaal sau saal se zyada se scientists ko uljha raha tha.
[6] Ab dawaiyon mein ye kyun matter karta hai? Ketamine lo. Ek version painkiller hai, doosra hallucinogenic street drug.
[7] Aur ibuprofen? Har tablet mein dono versions aadhe aadhe hote hain, par asar sirf ek ka hota hai.
[8] Isliye sahi version bana paana dawaiyon ko safer aur zyada effective bana sakta hai. Follow karo aur science ka maza lo!

## reel_2

Read these in order for this reel's narration:

[9] 51 percent se 99 percent. Ek chhota sa fark, ek molecule ne khud bada bana diya.
[10] Pehle Henri Kagan ne dikhaya ki catalyst mein ek chhoti si kami product mein bahut bada imbalance bana sakti hai. Isko kehte hain non-linear effect.
[11] Phir Kenso Soai ne ek kamaal ki reaction dikhayi, jahan product khud catalyst banne mein help karta hai.
[12] Matlab molecule khud ka hi aur zyada version banata hai. Isko kehte hain autocatalysis.
[13] Hamare body ke amino acids bhi sirf ek handedness wale hain. Ye kaam shayad batata hai ki aisa kaise possible ho sakta hai.
[14] Dhyan rahe, Soai reaction ye exact nahi batata ki life mein aisa hua kaise. Par ye dikhata hai ki ye ho sakta hai.
[15] Dono scientists 12 million Swedish crowns share karenge, aur 10 December ko Stockholm mein medal lenge. Science fans ke liye ye bada din hai!
