# Alternative hooks

- [Superlative] Chemistry ka Nobel 2026 mila ek aise mystery ko, jo sau saal se zyada se scientists ko uljha raha tha.
- [Statistic] Ibuprofen ki har tablet mein 50 percent hissa bekaar hota hai. Kyun? Nobel ka jawab yahin hai.
- [The Question] Aapke haath ek doosre ke mirror image hain, par overlap nahi hote. Toh molecules ka kya?
- [Someone Else's Result] Kagan aur Soai ko mila 2026 ka Chemistry Nobel. Aapki dawai se iska kya lena dena?
- [The Warning] Ek molecule ka mirror version jaan bacha sakta hai, doosra jaan le sakta hai.
